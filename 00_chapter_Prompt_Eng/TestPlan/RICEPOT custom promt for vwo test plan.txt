## 1. Objective

Validate that the CRM Portal authentication and logout functionality behaves according to REQ-01 through REQ-04.

* Proper logout and protection of authenticated content after logout.

### 2.2 Out of Scope

## 4. Test Approach

Testing will follow a **requirements-based functional approach**.

### Major test areas

1. **Login — Positive**

   * Verify successful login using an active account and valid credentials.

2. **Login — Negative**

   * Verify an incorrect password does not authenticate the user.
   * Verify empty required fields prevent authentication.

3. **Authenticated Session**

   * Verify successful authentication results in an authenticated state.
   * Verify access to protected content while authenticated.

4. **Logout**

   * Verify logout terminates the authenticated session.
   * Verify protected content cannot be accessed after logout.

5. **Regression**

   * Re-execute the core authentication and logout scenarios after relevant application changes.

**Major-step explanation:**
The approach converts the four requirements into testable functional areas rather than inventing additional application behavior.
